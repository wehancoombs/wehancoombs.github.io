Full profile

A teacher first

Philosopher and English teacher with a PhD and fifteen years of university teaching, from tutoring first-years to designing and running honours modules.

I joined the Department of Philosophy at the University of Pretoria as a tutor in 2010 and stayed on as assistant lecturer and then head lecturer, teaching first-year classes of more than five hundred students and designing four modules from scratch. From 2024 to 2026 I held a postdoctoral research fellowship at the University of Johannesburg on Platonic and African esotericism.

In 2026 I turned to the school classroom. During my Postgraduate Certificate in Education I taught Grade 10 and 11 English at Abbotts College Pretoria East and Hoërskool Garsfontein, and found the same pleasure there that I had found in the lecture hall: watching a room of people start to think together.

Alongside teaching I tutor, edit, and build study materials and simple web tools for learning.

For schools

Teaching English

FET Phase English, Home Language and First Additional Language. Literature, poetry, grammar and the craft of the essay.

Learners do their best thinking when they feel known and respected. I start from what they already know, then set a challenge they reason through together in discussion, and end by applying the new skill to their own lives, so they leave with ideas they can call their own.

Research

Plato, Neoplatonism and the esoteric

My doctoral thesis, Ainigma, read Plato’s esotericism in its historical context. My postdoctoral project at the University of Johannesburg widened the lens to Platonic and African esotericism. Running through both is an older question: what it means to practise philosophy as a way of life.
